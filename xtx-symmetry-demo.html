The top matrix and the left matrix meet in the corner: each product cell (i, j) is exactly where row i of the left matrix crosses column j of the top matrix. Click any product cell — its source row and column light up. Notice they hold the same numbers (one matrix is the other's transpose), which is why the product comes out symmetric.
